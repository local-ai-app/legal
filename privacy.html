1. At a glance

Snipit does not collect analytics, telemetry, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, or any personal data. All video import, AI segmentation, cropping, and rendering happens on your device. Nothing about your videos — frames, audio, region polygons, presets, or filenames — is transmitted to any server operated by us or a third party.

2. On-device processing

When you import a video into Snipit:

  • Frames are decoded locally and held inside the app's sandbox while you edit.
  • AI segmentation, manual tracing, and lasso selection all run on-device.
  • The 9:16 vertical clip is rendered locally and written to a file inside the app's sandbox.
  • If you choose to save the rendered clip to Photos, it is copied to your Photos library — still entirely on your device.

We have no ability to access, copy, or inspect any video you process with Snipit.

3. Photos library access

Snipit asks for two Photos permissions, each used only for the purpose stated when you grant it:

  • Read access — used to import the gameplay video you choose from your Photos library so it can be cropped. Snipit does not enumerate, scan, or index your library beyond the specific clip you select.
  • Add access — used to save a rendered 9:16 clip back to Photos when you tap "Save". Snipit only writes the output clip; it cannot read, modify, or delete other photos or videos.

Both permissions can be revoked at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Snipit.

4. What we collect

Nothing from you directly. The app does not integrate analytics SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, advertising SDKs, or attribution SDKs. We do not maintain user accounts, and we do not ask for your email, phone number, or any identifying information.

Saved presets, layout choices, export settings, and the most recent crop are stored locally on your device only.

5. Network activity

Snipit is designed to work offline. The app makes network requests only in these situations:

  • Optional AI model download. If you enable an advanced AI segmentation tier, the app downloads the required model files from a public content-delivery network on first use. No identifiers or account information are sent with this request beyond what any standard HTTPS file download includes. The basic AI tier ships with the app and requires no download.
  • Tapping an external link. If you tap a link in the app (for example, this policy), your browser opens the destination URL. That request is handled by your browser and the destination server, not by Snipit.

Beyond these, the app performs no background network activity.

6. Purchases

Snipit is free to use today. If a paid feature or subscription is introduced in a future version, payment will be handled exclusively through Apple's in-app purchase system using your Apple ID. We will not receive your name, email, credit-card details, billing address, or Apple ID. This policy will be updated to describe the specific purchase in detail before any paid feature ships.

7. Children's privacy

Snipit is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children. If you are under the age of majority in your jurisdiction, please use the app only with the involvement of a parent or guardian.

8. Data retention

Because Snipit does not transmit personal data to our servers, there is nothing about you on our side to retain or delete. Local content inside the app (imported video frames, saved presets, model files, rendered clips, cached data) is under your control and can be cleared at any time from within the app or by deleting the app from your device.

9. Third parties

Snipit uses on-device AI models and supporting components published by third parties under their respective licenses. These run entirely on your device and do not transmit your content to their authors. Optional model files, when downloaded, are fetched directly from publicly available hosts; the requests carry no Snipit-issued identifiers.

Details of these components, and links to the licenses that govern them, are listed in the app under Settings → Terms & Disclaimers → Third-Party Components.

10. Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data, most data-protection rights (access, correction, deletion, portability) are satisfied automatically: there is no personal data on our side to access, correct, delete, or export. If you have questions about your rights under applicable law (GDPR, CCPA, PIPEDA, or other frameworks), contact us using the address below and we will respond in good faith.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will also be reflected in the next version of the app. Continued use of Snipit after an update means you accept the updated policy.
