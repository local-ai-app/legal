1. Safety — not a substitute for sight

Kafeef is designed to help blind and low-vision users get spoken information about their surroundings. It is a supplementary aid only. It is not a mobility device, a navigation system, a medical device, or a substitute for your white cane, guide dog, orientation-and-mobility training, or assistance from sighted people.

Do not rely on Kafeef for decisions where an error could harm you or others. This includes, without limitation:

  • Crossing roads, judging traffic, or detecting moving vehicles, bicycles, or trains.
  • Detecting stairs, curbs, drop-offs, edges, holes, wet floors, glass, or other physical hazards.
  • Identifying medications, dosages, or medical instructions.
  • Identifying food allergens, expiry dates, or whether something is safe to eat or drink.
  • Handling money, signing documents, or any financial or legal decision.
  • Detecting fire, smoke, gas, electrical, or other emergency hazards, or responding to an emergency.
  • Navigating unfamiliar, hazardous, or fast-changing environments.

Always continue to use your primary mobility aids, your own judgement, and human assistance where appropriate. In an emergency, contact local emergency services — do not rely on the app.
