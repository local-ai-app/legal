1. At a glance

Kafeef does not collect analytics, telemetry, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, or any personal data. It has no user accounts and no servers of its own. All scene description, text reading, object finding, speech recognition, translation, and search happen on your device. Nothing about what your camera sees or what you ask — images, audio, text, or descriptions — is transmitted to any server operated by us or a third party.

2. On-device processing

When you point Kafeef at a scene and ask it to describe, read, find, or narrate:

  • The camera frame is analyzed locally by on-device machine-learning models to produce a spoken description.
  • Text is recognized locally (on-device OCR) and read aloud locally.
  • Your spoken questions are turned into text on your device, and answers are spoken back on your device.
  • Translation between English and Arabic happens locally.

We have no ability to access, copy, or inspect any image, sound, or description that Kafeef processes.

3. What we collect

Nothing from you directly. The app does not integrate analytics SDKs, crash-reporting SDKs, advertising SDKs, or attribution SDKs. We do not maintain user accounts, and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or any identifying information. Kafeef is free and contains no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

4. Camera, microphone & photos

Kafeef requests access to your camera, microphone, speech recognition, and (optionally) your photo library only to provide its core features:

  • Camera is active only while you are on a capture screen (Describe, Ask, Find, Read, or Walk). Frames are analyzed in real time on your device and are not saved, except for the small thumbnail you choose to keep in your memory journal (see below).
  • Microphone & speech recognition are used only while you hold a button to ask a question. Audio is converted to text on your device and is not stored or transmitted.
  • Photo library is accessed only if you choose to pick an existing photo for Kafeef to describe.

5. Your memory journal

So you can later ask "what did I see earlier?", Kafeef keeps an on-device journal of your past captures — a short English description plus a small thumbnail image of each. This journal is stored only inside the app's private sandbox on your device. It is never uploaded, shared, or synced. You can erase it at any time from Settings → Reset memory, and it is permanently deleted when you delete the app.

6. Network activity

Kafeef is designed to work fully offline after first setup. The app makes network requests only in these situations:

  • One-time model download. On first use, the app downloads the AI model file it needs from a public content-delivery network. No identifiers or account information are sent with this request beyond what any standard HTTPS file download includes.
  • Tapping an external link. If you open a link such as this policy, your browser opens the destination URL. That request is handled by your browser and the destination server, not by Kafeef.

Beyond these, the app performs no background network activity. There are no subscriptions or purchases, so there is no billing-related network traffic.

7. Children's privacy

Kafeef does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children. Because the app collects no personal data at all, it does not build profiles of, or target, any user. If you are under the age of majority in your jurisdiction, please use the app with the involvement of a parent or guardian.

8. Data retention

Because Kafeef does not transmit personal data to any server, there is nothing about you on our side to retain or delete. Local content inside the app — your memory journal, model files, and cached data — is under your control and can be cleared at any time from within the app or by deleting the app from your device.

9. Third parties

Kafeef uses open-source libraries and model weights published by third parties under their respective licenses. These are bundled in or downloaded by the app and run entirely on your device; they do not transmit your content to their authors.

Details of these components, and links to the licenses that govern them, are listed in the app under Settings → Terms & Disclaimers → Third-Party Components and in section 7 of the Terms.

10. Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data, most data-protection rights (access, correction, deletion, portability) are satisfied automatically: there is no personal data on our side to access, correct, delete, or export. If you have questions about your rights under applicable law (GDPR, CCPA, PIPEDA, or other frameworks), contact us using the address below and we will respond in good faith.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will also be reflected in the next version of the app. Continued use of Kafeef after an update means you accept the updated policy.
